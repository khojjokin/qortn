Who we are

Light on its wings. Solid at its core.

Qortn is a small, independent studio. We make apps that help people stay close to each other and to the groups they care about.

We start small, sweat the details, and only ship what we would happily use ourselves. Our name and our winged mark stand for the same idea: software that feels light, built on something solid.

Made with care

Every screen is designed, tested and polished by hand, on every device we ship to.

Private by design

We collect only what an app needs to work. Your account, your words and your photos stay yours.

Built for everyone

Light and dark themes, many languages and room for every ability, from the first version.

Get to know Sanctuary.

The Bible

A verse for every morning.

Start the day with a verse, read on at your own pace and keep your streak going.

Communities

A place for every group.

Prayer circles, choirs and ministries get their own page, with a cover, posts and events.

Map

See who is nearby.

Friends who choose to share their location show up on a map of your town.

Lessons

Study together, every week.

This week’s memory text and today’s reading, ready when you are.

Hymnal

Sing the old songs together.

Every hymn by its number, with the words verse by verse and a new hymn each day.

Made for the big screen too.

On a laptop or in any browser, Sanctuary spreads out. A community's page shows its cover, its pinned post and everything about it side by side.
