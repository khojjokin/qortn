Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy covers the Qortn website (this site). It is short because the site collects very little.

Who we are

This website is run by Kingsford K. Toku, trading as Qortn (“Qortn”, “we”, “us”). If you have a question about your privacy, email support@qortyn.com.

What this site is

A static website about Qortn and our apps. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms and no comments. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and the site does not set cookies or store anything in your browser.

What is collected when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives technical details from your browser when it serves a page, such as your IP address, the page asked for, the time, and your browser and device type. Cloudflare uses these to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and may keep them in its logs for a short time. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser asks Google for the font files, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google. See Google’s privacy policy.

We do not use this information to identify you, build a profile of you, or sell it.

The copy button

The email address in the footer has a button that copies the address to your clipboard. It only copies text on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you email us

If you write to support@qortyn.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply and to help you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that.

Our apps

Each Qortn app, including Community, has its own privacy policy and terms, which you can read inside the app. Links from this site to an app’s website take you to that site, which is covered by its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. Email us and we will help. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

This site is a general information site and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change how this site handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
